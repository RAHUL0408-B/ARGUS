## Structure (follows the organizers' mandatory format exactly)

Per the "Team Hackthecore" announcement requiring all teams to follow a fixed 9-section structure:

1. **Team Introduction & Problem Statement** - `team` slide
2. **The Problem** - `problem` slide
3. **Your Solution** - `solution` + `workflow` slides
4. **Project Architecture & Structure** - `architecture` slide
5. **Tech Stack** - `techstack` slide (software stack + honest tool-integration matrix)
6. **Features** - `features` + `safety` slides
7. **Feasibility** - `feasibility` slide (live proof stats as evidence)
8. **Limitations** - `limitations` slide (honest, specific)
9. **Future Scope** - `future` slide (closes the deck)

12 slides total (`cover` is a title slide before section 1). Each slide's eyebrow label states its section number explicitly for easy evaluation.

Content is drawn from `docs/PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, and the verified live results in `docs/ARGUS_Test_Report.pdf` - nothing on the slides is invented or unverified.
